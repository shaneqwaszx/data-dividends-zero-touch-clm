This list is an illustrative placeholder for the POC, not a confirmed bank policy. Check the accepted documents with your compliance team.

Documents commonly used to show who owns a company:
- Register of members (the company's shareholder register), showing each shareholder and how many shares they hold.
- Share certificates, together with the total number of shares issued.
- Register of registrable controllers, which names the people with significant control.
- A signed ownership structure chart, when shareholders are other companies; it should trace ownership up to the individuals at the top.
- For a corporate shareholder, the same kind of document for that company.

Whatever document is used, the UBO check needs it to show, for each owner, one of: an ownership percentage, a share count with the total shares, a capital amount, or a membership interest. Directors and officers are not treated as owners because of their role.
