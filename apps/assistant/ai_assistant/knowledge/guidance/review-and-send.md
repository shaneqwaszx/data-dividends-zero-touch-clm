Zero-touch does not mean no people: human review of exceptions is expected.

In this POC, KYC is evidence preparation. No separate KYC analyst, compliance approver or second final approver is required. The RM's Review & Send confirms the prepared submission; it is not regulatory approval.

Local risk results are advisory: LOW continues, MEDIUM warns and continues, HIGH may ask for RM attention.
