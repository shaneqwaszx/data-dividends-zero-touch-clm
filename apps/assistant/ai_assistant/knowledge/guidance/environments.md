Mock, Local, Sandbox, Partial, Dormant and Live are different kinds of evidence. Only Live comes from the real production service.

In this POC, ACRA and GoldTier data shown on a case is labelled with where it came from (for example ACRA (MOCK)). A mock or sandbox result is not proof that the real service would return the same.
