title: How policy settings are changed
stages: Policy
source: docs/decisions/DECISION_LOG.md#D-064
illustrative: false
---
Policy settings are changed by maker-checker: HR drafts a change with a reason, and a Director approves or rejects it. The person who proposed a change cannot decide it.

An approved change becomes a new active version; older versions are kept. A case keeps the policy that applied when it started.
