### F2 — P2: A→B→A reactivates superseded independent ownership

Location: `apps/backend/app/services/ownership_analysis.py:1072–1082`.

For a non-registry provider, matching the current UEN returns True immediately, bypassing intervening identity corrections. Required correction: distinguish harmless same-UEN verification from an intervening identity change for every relevant source. An A→B→A analysis must remain historical until explicitly refreshed under the current identity epoch. Preserve independent evidence for safe same-UEN verification, and prove valid replacement/retirement in memory and physical repositories.
