## Why saves feel slow and optimization decision

Identity and ownership saves are more than field persistence: they validate and persist new evidence/history, then synchronously invoke the authoritative downstream workflow. Legal-name save re-runs document/reconciliation/readiness work. Classification save performs authoritative classification/currentness and projection work before final review becomes usable. Final confirmation persists ledger stages in order, then Shell, Policy and exact-ID readback. Recovery resolution persists verified evidence without replay; safe continuation finalizes the retained intent. These costs occur inside the owning synchronous request rather than after a quick optimistic save.

The representative identity request made 255 reads/30 writes with 249 whole-case decodes; ownership made 181/11 with 176 decodes; classification 122/4 with 117 decodes; confirmation 157/31 with 141 decodes. The exclusive repository plus full-envelope decode/encode/validation work dominates these traces. Mongo driver round trips contribute but are not the whole cost. This serial loop shows no material lock contention (representative waits 0.002-0.008 ms), no zero-match CAS update and no driver failures. Auth/dispatch were small. Inclusive response projection can include its own repository reads/decodes; its duration must not be added again to the waterfall. Nested observer duplicates in early traces do not prove redundant business recomputation.

The PDF example spent 854.88 ms in local OCR plus 418.99 ms in separately instrumented validation/currentness and persistence around document processing. Some validation/framework cost has only owner-method attribution; it remains UNKNOWN at finer granularity. Mock provider work is minimal and does not establish real provider wait. Removing full-envelope reads or introducing request caching would require proving cross-writer currentness/CAS at each existing owner boundary. That proof and persistence-boundary refactor exceed a safe, evidence-supported optimization in this task.

**No safe bounded optimization was justified by the measurements; the dominant latency is necessary synchronous persistence/orchestration work.** No product performance change was applied, and no before/after product improvement is claimed. Direct timing observer refinements correct measurement contamination only. Current progress banners and intake progress communicate the longer compound operation; no repeated >2-second silent save was observed requiring new feedback source changes. A later separately scoped performance design may investigate repeated envelope validation while preserving history, reauthorization, CAS/currentness, durability, ordered target calls and no replay.
