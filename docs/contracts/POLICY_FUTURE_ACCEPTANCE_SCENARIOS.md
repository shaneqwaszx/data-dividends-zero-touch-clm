# Conditional Future Policy Acceptance Scenarios

These scenarios are future contracts only. They are not active requirements, skipped
product tests, or evidence that the sponsor selected an option.

## Supplied risk

IF the sponsor confirms LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH is externally supplied, THEN a future change
must validate the exact vocabulary and producer, retain provenance, reject unknown
values, and prove no calculated score is introduced.

IF the sponsor additionally confirms HIGH blocks readiness or requires EDD, THEN the
future change must prove the exact transition, authorized reviewer action, evidence,
resume behavior, and no effect for values not covered by that decision.

## Review and separation of duties

IF the sponsor confirms four-eyes review, THEN a future change must define first and
second actors, prohibit any forbidden self-review path in the backend authority owner,
retain the approved evidence, and test inaccessible, stale, concurrent, and replayed
mutations.

IF self-review is permitted for a bounded disposition, THEN the implementation must
limit that permission to the confirmed role/action and must not infer final approval.

## Assignment and priority

IF the sponsor confirms assignment/reassignment, THEN a future change must prove the
assignment authority, team cardinality, reason/history, visibility, and safe concurrent
mutation semantics.

IF the sponsor confirms priority values 1–3 and 1 is highest, THEN a future change must
define migration/compatibility from nullable P1–P10 and prove ordering without adding
SLA, escalation, routing, notification, or risk effects unless separately confirmed.

## Ready-for-Approval

IF the sponsor confirms Ready-for-Approval is a distinct GoldTier action, THEN a future
change must use the confirmed operation/state, prerequisites, authentication, idempotency,
failure and recovery contract, and must keep Mock submission distinct.

IF Ready-for-Approval is automatic after a confirmed call sequence, THEN the future
change must prove that exact sequence and evidence; it must not infer final approval.

## Source, document, UBO, retention, and handoff

IF the sponsor confirms source precedence by field, THEN future reconciliation must
apply only those field/condition rules and retain the losing evidence and decision.

IF the sponsor confirms a final UBO threshold/control rule, THEN a future change must
prove Decimal calculation, graph uncertainty behavior, legal terminology, and review
boundary for the confirmed rule.

IF the sponsor confirms retention/reviewer-access requirements, THEN a future change
must specify storage, deletion, access, audit, and recovery effects before schema or
activity claims change.

IF the sponsor confirms the production Zero-Touch/GoldTier handoff, THEN a future
change must prove the exact deployment owner, handoff point, transferred state/evidence,
identity, target continuation, and failure ownership without promoting Mock evidence to
Live.
